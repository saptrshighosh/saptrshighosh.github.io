Current research

Jan 2026 – Present

University of Oklahoma

Graduate Research Assistant

ML for spectral classification

NanoSpectral BioDiagnostics Lab · Dr. Rupa Haldavnekar

I develop Python pipelines for approximately 5,000 Raman / surface-enhanced Raman spectroscopy (SERS) spectra, linking experimental sensing with classification and biomarker analysis.

  • Implement ALS baseline correction, Savitzky–Golay smoothing, normalization, feature extraction, and model evaluation.
  • Compare SVM, Random Forest, neural networks, and discriminant-analysis methods; use PCA and UMAP to examine data structure.
  • Evaluate with ROC/AUC and confusion matrices. Reported 95% classification accuracy across four cancer cell lines, using biological sample-level splitting and training-only fitting of data-dependent preprocessing and model tuning.
  • Connect computational analysis with SERS nanosensor fabrication, biological interpretation, manuscripts, and an invention disclosure.

The 95% result describes this research dataset and evaluation; it is not a claim of clinical diagnostic accuracy.

  • Python
  • scikit-learn
  • Spectral processing
  • Model evaluation
Master’s research

2024 – 2025

University of Oklahoma

Graduate Research Assistant

EEG analysis & cognitive inhibition

Product and Process Design Lab · Dr. Zahed Siddique

Analyzed EEG data from 29 participants in Stroop-task experiments to investigate how thermal conditions affect cognitive inhibition.

  • Processed signals in MATLAB using event-related potentials and time-frequency decomposition.
  • Contributed to neurocognitive experiment and ERP task design.
  • Applied ANOVA, Welch’s t-tests, Pearson correlation, and Benjamini–Hochberg correction; identified significant temperature-dependent differences in inhibitory performance (adjusted p < 0.05).
